# Codebook

These are the rules the author followed to label every patent, figure and aircraft in file 32. It is the only codebook of the dataset. The labels were typed in the wizard, a web page with one screen per stage, and the wizard has the locks of section 7 built in, so a locked combination cannot be saved.

A **dimension** is the question being answered and an **option** is one of its answers. A dimension that applies only to certain architectures or components has the case where it applies in brackets after its name. Components that an aircraft carries more than once, such as wings, boom sets and propulsors, are recorded one unit at a time.

Sections:

1. The six stages
2. The five principles and the two escapes
3. How the stages are applied (the rules of the procedure)
4. Patent triage (T1) and image triage (T2)
5. Architecture (G1)
6. Morphology: structural (M1), aerodynamic (M2) and propulsive (M3)
7. Locks
8. References
